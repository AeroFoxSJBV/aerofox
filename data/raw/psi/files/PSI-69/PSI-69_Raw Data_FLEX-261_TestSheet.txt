Free-floating methanol droplet in a 0.21/0.49/0.30 O2/N2/He, 0.70 atm ambient environment.  The droplet drifts NW in the HiBMS FOV after deployment, hits the igniter and then drifts quickly S after ignition.  The droplet leaves the HiBMS FOV shortly after ignition so only a small portion of the droplet burning history is captured.  It also leaves the color camera FOV.
