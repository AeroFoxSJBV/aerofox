Fiber-supported heptane droplet with 3 mm/sec translation burning in a 0.34/0.66 O2/N2, 0.70 atm ambient environment.  There is significant transverse and axial oscillations on the fiber during the entire burn although the droplet remains within the HiBMS FOV for the entire test.
