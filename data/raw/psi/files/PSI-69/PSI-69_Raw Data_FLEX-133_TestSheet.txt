Free-floating heptane droplet in a 0.24/0.56/0.20 O2/N2/CO2, 1.0 atm ambient environment.  The droplet drifts NW and out of the HiBMS FOV before the end of the test (about half way). The extinction droplet diameter is the droplet diameter when the flame disrupts and is estimated from the burning rate constant while the droplet is within the HiBMS FOV and the time of extinction from either the LLUV or color camera views.
