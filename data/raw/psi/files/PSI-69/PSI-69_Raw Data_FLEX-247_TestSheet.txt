COMMENTS:

Free-floating methanol droplet burning in a 0.16/0.59/0.25 O2/N2/He, 1.0 atm ambient environment.  The droplet is nearly motionless after deployment and ignition and remains in the FOV of all the cameras for the entire recording time.  It is difficult to say whether this is diffusive or radiative extinction.  It is likely near the flammability boundary.  The MDCA Color Camera did not record anything beyond droplet deployment on-board for this test.
