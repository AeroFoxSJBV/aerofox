COMMENTS:

Free-floating heptane droplet burning in a nominally air, 0.21/0.79 O2/N2, 1.0 atm ambient environment.  The droplet drifts SE in the HiBMS FOV after deployment, hits the igniter and begins to drift N.  The drift velocity is relatively high and it drifts out of the HiBMS FOV relatively quickly after ignition.  It also drifts out of the LLUV and Color Camera FOVs before the end of the test.  The flame oscillates briefly in the middle of the burn and then becomes steady and burns to a disruptive extinction.  The flame is very dim in the Color Camera FOV with the leading edge of the flame (in the direction of the droplet drift) much brighter than the trailing edge.
